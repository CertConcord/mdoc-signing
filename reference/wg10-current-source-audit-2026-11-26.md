# ISO/IEC 18013-5 public-source check — 2026-11-26

**Finding.** No newer *public WG10 working text* or public WG10 algorithm-suite selection found here changes the project's classification of ML-DSA `issuerAuth` and `DeviceAuth` as an **experimental mdoc extension**. ISO has advanced edition 2 to a DIS, but its algorithm clauses were not available in the reviewed public repository; this check cannot establish what the DIS selects.
